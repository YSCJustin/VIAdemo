TL;DR: Just use the website

Imagine this scenario in your head right now on the spot: it is 12:30pm, the lunch bell has just rung. Students slowly migrate to either the canteen or either entrances. You choose the latter. You go to the main entrance with your friends to eat lunch. Out of nowhere, one of your friends asks a rather intriguing question:「食乜嘢啊？！ (interrobang)」You are flabbergasted by this sudden inquiry, leaving you dumbfounded. What restaurant? What cuisine? How far do I have to travel? You turn on your phone, open Google Maps, and search for restaurants nearby. You are presented with a list of restaurants. Full of options, yet you couldn't pick any one of them. You look at the watch on your left wrist and realise you are a prisoner of time. Time is ticking, and you and your friends still haven't decided on what to eat. Perplexed, you hope for a solution to this conundrum.

In this scenario, this website would be your saving grace. Any other scenario than this would render the website useless, a waste of electricity, and possibly occupying some of your mobile phone's resources. Yikes.

Back to reality, you are probably ruminating about "why should I care about this website?" The short answer: you shouldn't. The long answer: you shouldn't if you are great at making decisions. If you have aboulomania, consider using this website once a while. Using this website has two outcomes: either you do find a random restaurant and you eat there, end of story, or there are voices in your head telling you to keep rolling the dice and find another restaurant till the end of time, in which your friends slowly loses hope and make a beeline for some restaurants they have been to myriads of times. Either way, it is a win-win: at the end of the day you still found a restaurant.

What has this website achieved? Let's look at the bigger picture, the grand scheme of things. This website could theoretically, potentially, possibly, perhaps, maybe achieve the 8th Goal of Sustainable Development, which is "Decent Work And Economic Growth". Think about it hard, connect the dots. Hypothetically, if this website returns a random restaurant that one would have never stumbled upon if one weren't utilising this website, it would boost those random restaurants' profit. Ultimately eliminating the need for users, especially indecisive ones, to rely on mainstream options. So, all things considered, this website is technically changing the world by making society a better place.
